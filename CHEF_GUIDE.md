# ChefOS — User Guide 🍳

Welcome to ChefOS — your kitchen's recipes, inventory, costs, events, and a smart
assistant, all in one app that runs right on your computer. No internet required
for daily use.

---

## 1. Install it (one time)

1. Double-click **`ChefOS_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe`**.
2. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click **More info → Run anyway**
   (this is normal for new apps).
3. Follow the installer (Next → Install). When it finishes, ChefOS is in your
   **Start Menu**.

> The first time it opens, **Windows Firewall** may ask to allow ChefOS — click
> **Allow access** (this is only needed if you'll use it from your phone later).

---

## 2. First time you open it — activate

1. Launch **ChefOS** from the Start Menu.
2. You'll see an **activation screen**. Type the **license key** you were given
   (it looks like `CHEF-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) and press **Activate**.
3. That's it — you'll never see this screen again on this computer.

> Activation needs internet **once**. After that, ChefOS works fully offline.

---

## 3. A quick tour (the menu on the left)

| Menu item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your kitchen at a glance — costs, alerts, upcoming events. |
| **Mgzz Assistant** | Ask it anything (see section 4). The star of the show. |
| **Recipes** | Your recipes, their ingredients and cost per portion. |
| **Items** | Finished products made from several recipes + ingredients (e.g. a platter). |
| **Simulate** | "If I cook this for N people…" — see cost & whether you have the stock, then cook. |
| **Cooked Stock** | Batches you've already cooked and stored. |
| **Cook History** | Everything you've cooked, with the option to undo/cancel. |
| **Waste Log** | What you've thrown away and what it cost you. |
| **Inventory** | Your stock levels, with low/expiring warnings. |
| **Ingredients** | Add/edit ingredients, prices, suppliers. |
| **Categories** | Organize ingredients (Produce, Dairy, Dry Goods…). |
| **Events** | Plan a party/catering job — dishes, guests, total cost. |
| **Setup Items / Vendors** | Equipment you rent and the suppliers you buy from. |
| **Settings** | Your brand, phone access, backups (see sections 5–7). |

## 4. The Mgzz Assistant — your kitchen brain

Click **Mgzz Assistant** and just type (or tap the 🎤 mic and **speak**). It uses
your real recipes, prices and stock. Try:

- **"Make Pasta Carbonara for 20 people"** → exact ingredient amounts, total cost,
  cost per person, a **shopping list** of what you're short on, and step-by-step.
- **"Plan an event for 50 guests"** → scales your dishes, totals the cost, combined
  shopping list.
- **"What can I cook right now?"** → everything you have the stock for.
- **"Build my shopping list"** → what to restock and how much.
- **"What's urgent?"** → out-of-stock and expiring items.
- **"Pasta Carbonara at 50%"** → the sell price for a 50% profit margin.
- **"Which recipes cost the most?"**, **"What's my inventory worth?"**

Every answer has a **🖨 Print / PDF** button — great for prep sheets and shopping lists.

---

## 5. Make it yours (Settings → Brand)

- Set your **restaurant/kitchen name** and **logo** — they show in the sidebar and
  on printouts.
- Set your **Target profit margin %** — the assistant uses it to suggest sell prices.
- Rename the **assistant** to whatever you like.

---

## 6. Use it on your phone or tablet (same WiFi)

1. On the computer: **Settings → Mobile Access → turn on**.
2. A **QR code** and an address (like `http://192.168.1.5:8000`) appear.
3. On your phone (same WiFi), **scan the QR with the camera** — ChefOS opens with
   all your data.
4. Turn it **off** when you're done to keep things private.

---

## 7. Your data is safe (Settings → Backups)

- **Automatic:** a backup is saved every day (the last 30 are kept) — nothing to do.
- **Export Backup → Desktop:** makes a dated `.zip` on your Desktop you can keep
  or email yourself.
- **Restore:** pick any daily backup (or a `.zip` you saved) to roll back. It asks
  you to confirm first.
- **Cloud Backup (Google Drive):** click **Connect Google Drive**, sign in once,
  and a copy is uploaded to your own Drive every day (last 7 kept). You can
  **Backup Now** or **Restore from Google Drive** any time.

---

## 8. Everyday tips

- **Add stock:** Inventory → find the ingredient → **Restock** (enter amount + expiry).
- **Cook something:** Simulate → pick the recipe → set portions → **Cook**
  (this deducts the ingredients automatically, oldest stock first).
- **Cook ahead:** Simulate → **Cook & Store to Stock** (keeps it as cooked stock).
- **Costs always update** when you change an ingredient's price.

---

## 9. If something looks off

- **App won't open / blank window:** close it fully and reopen from the Start Menu.
- **"Mobile access is off" on your phone:** turn it on in Settings → Mobile Access,
  and make sure the phone is on the **same WiFi**.
- **Assistant says it can't reach the service:** wait a second and ask again (it's
  just starting up).
- **Need help or a new license:** contact support.

Enjoy ChefOS — happy cooking! 👨‍🍳
